Initial Findings
The analysis of DWD temperature data for January indicates a noticeable long-term warming trend.
A comparison of historical periods shows:

- Mean January temperature before 1950: approximately -0.79 °C
- Mean January temperature from 1950 onwards: approximately 0.17 °C
This corresponds to a difference of almost 1 °C.
Yearly temperatures show substantial variability, which makes long-term trends difficult to identify from raw observations alone. A 10-year moving average was therefore used to reveal broader temperature developments over time.
